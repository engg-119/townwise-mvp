## Decisions

### 1. "Your calendars" opens a popover (request 1)

Owner, 2026-11-15: "if I press your calendars on the calendar page, it should show a popover instead of a component in the actual page." This reverses the owner's 2026-11-14 choice of one header button with its panel under the row (spec D33, P28's Your calendars bullet, connect-owner-calendar design decision 11). The button, its name ("Your calendars (N)"), its place in the header row and everything inside the panel (the list, the messages line, Import a calendar file, Add a link and its form, "How importing works", the read-only line, the Remove dialog) stay as connect-owner-calendar specifies; only where that content shows changes.

*Claude's call: a shared `Popover` (`client/src/portal/ui/Popover.tsx`, one file for web and native).* React Native's `Modal`, `transparent`, `animationType="none"`, as `ConfirmDialog` uses it, so on web react-native-web traps focus inside it, Escape closes it through `onRequestClose` and focus returns to the opener on close, and on native Android's back button closes it; its `onDismiss` passes through, so a page can move focus once the popover has gone (as `ConfirmDialog`'s does). Inside the `Modal` the card comes first and the backdrop after it, so the focus trap's first focusable element is in the card: on open focus lands on the card's first control ("Close" in Your calendars, the form's Kind in the note form). The backdrop is a plain `View` (never a `Pressable`, which is always focusable on web) that fills the window absolutely under the card (the card has the higher `zIndex`), transparent (no dim) so the page stays visible, with `aria-hidden`, `accessible={false}` and `importantForAccessibility="no-hide-descendants"`, so it is never a tab stop or a screen reader stop; it takes presses through the responder props (`onStartShouldSetResponder`, `onResponderRelease`) and closes the popover only when the popover allows that. The card is `role="dialog"` named by its heading, a `panel` fill, a 1px `border` outline, the `shadow` token, square corners and 16px padding, at most a given width (440px for Your calendars, 400px for the note form) and never wider than the window less 32px. From 641px wide, placement comes from the opener's `measureInWindow` (both platforms) and `useWindowDimensions`, measured on open and again when the window size changes: it opens 8px below the opener when the room below is at least 320px or at least the room above, and otherwise 8px above it; its height is at most the room between the opener and that edge less 24px (the 8px gap and a 16px margin), and its content scrolls inside it beyond that (a `ScrollView` with `keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled"`). Horizontally its right edge lines up with the opener's when the opener's center is in the right half of the window, and its left edge otherwise, kept 16px from either window edge. Below 641px (a phone, web or native) both popovers hold text fields that the keyboard would cover if the card sat under a button in the lower half, so the card is pinned to the top of the window instead: 16px from the top and from either side, the window's width less 32px. At every width the window's usable bottom is the keyboard's top while the keyboard shows (`Keyboard` show and hide events on native, both iOS and Android; `window.visualViewport`'s height and its resize event on web), and placement is measured again when it changes, so the card's height never runs under the keyboard and its own scroll view brings the focused field into view. Nothing animates.
*Alternatives:* an absolutely positioned card inside the page (the chat's History list, `chat/ui/ChatHeader.tsx`). Rejected: inside the desktop day panel, which is sticky, a card taller than the room under its button can never be scrolled into view (decision 2), and on Android a touch outside the parent's box is not delivered (the reason History keeps native in the flow). A centered dialog was not what the owner asked for.

*Claude's call: what survives closing.* A `Modal`'s children unmount when it closes, so the state that survives closing the panel today (the messages line, the help's open state, whether the add-a-link form is open, and its Name and Calendar link fields and their problems) moves up into `YourCalendars`' `Calendars`, which stays mounted with the button, and so does the link add's action (`add`, with its pending state and its in-flight guard), so no remount can offer an enabled "Add link" while a post is still out. A half-typed link and a shown message are still there when the popover opens again, as they are after closing the panel today. Since unmounting no longer clears the form, a 201 and Cancel each clear Name, Calendar link and their problems explicitly; the address is never logged or shown again.

*Claude's call: the button's semantics.* The outline `Button` keeps `expanded` (`aria-expanded`, `accessibilityState.expanded`) and gains `hasPopup` (`aria-haspopup="dialog"`, added to `WebAria` in `ui/aria.ts`); `aria-controls` names the dialog only while it is open, since a closed popover is not in the document. The popover has the heading "Your calendars" and a "Close" text button at its top. A press outside closes it, except while the Remove dialog it opened is open (that dialog is a second `Modal` over it).

*Claude's call: an answer always lands in the open popover.* While a link add, a file import or a Refresh now runs (a link add can take about 10 seconds), "Close" is disabled in place and Escape, Android's back button and a press outside do nothing, as `ConfirmDialog` does while its action runs ("so its answer always lands in the open dialog") and as Add link's own Cancel already does. So no answer, a refused link above all, can arrive while the popover is closed and go unseen; the button says what is happening ("Adding…", "Importing…", "Refreshing…"). *Alternative:* let it close and announce a late answer in a live region outside it. Rejected: a sighted owner would still see nothing, and a refused link's words belong beside its form.

*Claude's call: focus.* Opening it moves focus to its "Close" button (the card's first control, react-native-web's `Modal`); closing it returns focus to "Your calendars". The rules of connect-owner-calendar decision 11's *Focus* stay inside it ("Name" on Add a link, back to "Add a link" on Cancel, the messages line after an add, a Remove or a 404). Since every answer lands while the popover is open, the web check that focus is still in the panel before the messages line takes it goes.

*Correction while building it (group 1).* On native a `Modal` covers the whole screen, status bar included, so "16px from the window's top" would put a phone's card under an iPhone's status bar: the usable top is the safe area's top inset (0 on web), the card sits 16px under it, and with no keyboard the usable bottom is the window's bottom less the safe area's bottom inset. On Android the `Modal` is `statusBarTranslucent` and `navigationBarTranslucent`, so it shares the window coordinates its opener is measured in. `Popover` also takes a `nativeID` (what the opener's `aria-controls` names while it is open), an optional `closeButton` (the "Close" beside the heading, disabled while `busy`) and `busy` itself (group 2's task allowed for it), and it draws the heading from `label`, so each popover's heading is the dialog's only one. In `YourCalendars.tsx` the `hidden` style stays for "How importing works" (its help is mounted while hidden, so it can describe the import); only the panel's use of it went. The add-a-link form opened by "Add a link" takes focus in Name as it mounts, but shown again as the popover opens it does not (nor does "Add a link" after Cancel), so focus lands on Close and returns to the button on close. Two tests outside group 1's list looked up the old panel and broke with it: `calendar.test.web.tsx` and `calendar-views.test.web.tsx` now find the dialog and close the popover before using the page again (group 1 edited only those lookups).

*Correction after review (group 1).* On web react-native-web's window height is the visual viewport's (its height times its `scale`), which a phone's or tablet's keyboard shrinks, while the `Modal` is `position: fixed` over the layout viewport, which iOS Safari and Chrome on Android leave at its full height; a card above its opener, placed by its `bottom`, so landed one keyboard height too low, under the keyboard. The card and the backdrop now sit in a box at the window's top as tall as the height placement uses (on web the layout viewport's, `document.documentElement.clientHeight`; on native the window's), and on web the keyboard's top is the visual viewport's bottom in layout coordinates (its `offsetTop` plus its height times its `scale`, read again on its `resize` and `scroll`), so a pan to a focused field counts and a pinch zoom shrinks nothing. Above an opener that the keyboard covers, the card ends 16px above the keyboard's top instead of 8px above the opener, so no part of it is under the keyboard. From 641px a popover that opens with no opener to measure (its anchor's ref is null) is pinned at the top as below 641px, instead of staying hidden for a measurement that never comes. Pinned by `test/portal/ui/popover.test.web.tsx`. Comments in `IcsImport.web.tsx`, `IcsImport.tsx`, `calendars.ts` and `CalendarPage.tsx` that still describe the removed panel are outside group 1's files; task 2.6 moves them to the popover.

*Claude's call: the row.* With no full-width panel item, Toolbar's trailing slot holds only the button, so the header row is one 44px line from 1024px and two at 375px, as the skeleton already draws it (`skeleton-calendar-calendars`), and the skeleton is unchanged. calendar-views' "The header is one 44px row with the controls in a fixed order" gains a MODIFIED delta here, so the archived spec has no full-width line under the row.

### 2. "Add an event" opens a popover (request 2)

Owner, 2026-11-15: "same for add an event. make this a popover" (the day panel's "Add an event" and the form it opens).

*Claude's call: Edit and Make a note too.* All three open the same note form (`NoteForm`) today, in the same place, so all three open it in the same `Popover`, anchored to the button that was pressed, with the heading "Add an event", "Edit note" or "Make a note". The panel never changes shape while a form is open, and "Add an event" stays in the panel.

*Claude's call: closing.* The note form's popover closes on Cancel, Escape (Android's back button), a save, and the existing 404 path (`gone`); a press outside it does nothing, since that would throw away a half-filled form. *Orchestrator's call:* while a save or a delete runs ("Saving…"), Cancel is disabled in place and Escape and Android's back button do nothing, as in Your calendars (decision 1), so the answer always lands in the open popover and never on an unmounted form. Focus returns to the opener; when the opener is gone after a 404 (an Edit whose note was deleted), focus moves to the panel's notice, which says "This note isn't available.", through the repo's own mechanism for an outcome that took the opener away: the page is wrapped in `MessageFocus` (`ui/messageFocus.tsx`), `gone` marks the end, and the popover's `onDismiss` calls `focusMessage()` once the `Modal` has handed focus back, as YourCalendars' Remove does; the notice (`Notice`, which `takesFocus`) then takes it. Any change of the selected day closes the form, whatever made it: `select` sets `form` to null as today, and the page also sets `form` to null during render when `selected` changes, beside its `seen` update (`if (seen !== selected)`), so a `?day=` change that doesn't come through `select` (browser back or forward to another Calendar entry, or a link; a day click's `setParams` replaces the history entry, so it adds none) closes it too, and a new note's form never remounts over another day with the old day's draft.

*Claude's call: one heading.* `NoteForm` (`locations/NoteForm.tsx`) draws its own `Panel` region ("Add a note" or "Edit this note") with an eyebrow and a heading, which inside the popover's card would be a box in a box with two headings that disagree. It gains an optional `bare` prop that drops its `Panel`, eyebrow and heading and renders the rest unchanged; the popover passes it, so the popover's heading ("Add an event", "Edit note" or "Make a note") is the only heading and names the dialog. Notes and TownwiseAI's proposal card keep the form as it is.

*Claude's call: what moves.* `CalendarDay` loses its `form` prop and its in-place swap; `onAdd`, `onEdit` and `onMakeNote` also hand the page the pressed button as the anchor. `CalendarPage` renders the popover with the `NoteForm` it builds today, keyed as today, and its save, notice and reload rules are unchanged (spec P28: "Saved." until the forecast reads again). Under view-as the three buttons stay disabled in place, so the popover never opens.

*Correction while building it (group 2).* `MessageFocus` wraps the day panel and its popover, not the whole page. It focuses the message that appeared or changed in the update that asks, or else the first one that offers in tree order; the notice `gone` sets is already shown by the time the popover's `onDismiss` asks, so with the page wrapped, a page notice above the grid ("Townwise hasn't published a forecast…", or a failed forecast, list or stored-events read) took focus instead of "This note isn't available.". Inside the panel's own `MessageFocus` that notice is the only message (the grid, the week, the filters and the header row hold none, and "Your calendars" has its own). The mark that the opener went is set in the save's 404 branch, not in `gone`, since `gone` also serves Delete's 404, which closes the delete dialog with the popover already closed, so a mark there would outlive it and move focus on the next ordinary close; opening the form clears it as well. The save's pending state is `NoteForm`'s own, so `bare` also disables its Cancel while the save runs, and the page knows a save is out from its `save` callback (`saving`, set around the write), which is the popover's `busy`; a delete never runs while the popover is open (its dialog opens from the panel, which the popover's `Modal` covers), so `busy` is the save alone. The three openers are one `Opener` in `CalendarDay.tsx` (a `Button` that hands its own ref to `onPress`), since Edit and Make a note repeat per row, and the page copies the pressed button's host into its own anchor ref. `calendar.test.native.tsx` needed no change: it finds the form's fields on the whole screen, not inside "Selected day". Pinned by `test/portal/calendar/note-popover.test.web.tsx` (its 404 tests, one under the no-forecast notice) and `note-popover.test.native.tsx`.

*Correction after review (group 2).* A 404 is not the only save that takes the opener away: an edit that moves the note off the selected day (its dates or weekdays) closes the popover, focus goes back to its Edit, and the notes' read again then drops the note from the day and its Edit with it, so focus fell to the page body. `onSaved` now sets the same mark when the form was an edit and the saved note no longer covers the selected day (`noteCovers`, the panel's own test), so the "Saved." notice takes focus once the popover has gone. Add an event and Make a note keep their openers whatever day the note lands on, and a note's Edit shows only while "Your notes" is on, a filter the popover's `Modal` keeps out of reach, so the edit's dates are the whole test. Pinned by `note-popover.test.web.tsx`'s saved-edit test.

### 4. Today on the left (request 4)

Owner, 2026-11-15: "today should always load to be on the left day." Decided: Home's "This week in {Town}" strip and the Calendar's Week view both start on today when they load, today plus the next 6 days, not Monday to Sunday; Home's wording changes from "this week" to "the next 7 days"; the Calendar's week no longer matches a month-grid row, so it reads its own days.

*Claude's call: the Calendar's weeks are 7-day blocks counted from today.* The week shown is the block that holds the selected day: it starts on `today + 7 * floor((selected - today) / 7)` (new `weekStartFor(selected, today)` in `grid.ts`, calendar dates only, as the grid's). With no `?day=` the selected day is today, so the week starts on today; a link or reload at `?day=` shows the block that holds that day, so the days never shift under the person. Previous week and Next week still move the selected day 7 days (`stepWeek`), so they step whole blocks; they are disabled in place when the next block lies wholly outside the 25 months. The URL gains nothing. *Alternatives:* start the week on the selected day itself (rejected: every day click would move the columns under the pointer); keep Monday weeks after the first (rejected: Next week from the first block would overlap it).

*Claude's call: keys.* Left and Right move a day and cross into the next or previous block; Up and Down in the columns move 7 days, so they stay in the same column; Home and End go to the block's first and last day (Ctrl or Cmd with them, the same); PageUp and PageDown step the selected day's own month as today, and the block follows the new day. The month grid's keys and Home's grid's are unchanged. `gridMove` takes the block's first day in its `at` for the week and agenda layouts only (`at` is a union on `layout`), so the month grid's call in `CalendarGrid.web.tsx` is unchanged.

*Claude's call: labels.* The week label and the failed-forecast notice name the block: "Nov 15 to 21, 2026", "Nov 29 to Dec 5, 2026", "Dec 27, 2026 to Jan 2, 2027" (`weekLabel` takes the block's first day). Column heads already name their weekday ("Sun 15"), and today's column keeps its "Today" tag.

*Claude's call: reads.* The week view reads over the shown grid's range (`gridRange`) when the block's days inside the 25 months (`weekRange(start, bounds)`, the block clamped to them) all lie inside it, so switching views reads nothing and a week inside the grid is covered at once, and over `weekRange` itself when they don't (a block that runs past the grid's last day). Comparing the clamped block, not its raw 7 days, matters at the 25 months' ends: with today Nov 11, 2026, the block of Oct 29 to Nov 4, 2025 clamps to Nov 1 to 4, which November 2025's grid (Nov 1 to Dec 7, 2025) already covers, so it reads nothing more. One function, `readRangeFor(view, month, weekStart, bounds)` in `grid.ts`, gives the range every read of the page uses (the town calendar, the sales days through `pastRangeFor`, the stored events and the forecast, at most 42 days each); the page's reads are keyed by that range's `from` and `to` instead of the grid's month, and `CalendarInput.month` becomes `covered`, the range its reads were made for, which `buildGrid` and `buildWeek` use to tell covered days from pending ones. While a new read runs, the days the kept reads cover show their data, dimmed, as a month change does today. *Alternative:* the week always reads its own 7 days. Rejected: every switch between Month and Week would then read twice (the grid's 42 days, then the week's 7), for days the page already has.

*Home: the strip is today and the next 6 days.* `thisWeek` builds its rows from today (its `monday` and `sunday` become `first` and `last`); the strip, the table, the past-years rows, the periods, the first event and the summary follow, since they are made from those rows. The words (Claude's call on the exact ones): the block "The next 7 days in {Town}", its title "What's on the calendar, and how these days went before", the line "On the calendar in the next 7 days: {title} ({weekday}), and {n} more." and its empty forms ("Nothing on {Town}'s researched calendar in the next 7 days.", "No holiday or town event on {Town}'s researched calendar in the next 7 days.", and the same without the town), the table names "The next 7 days, day by day" and "These days in past years". *Claude's call: the tile follows the strip.* "This week, last year" sums the strip's own last-year days, so it becomes "The next 7 days, last year" with "Same days last year, {Sun, Nov 16} to {Sat, Nov 22, 2025}. From all 7 days." and "Fewer than 5 of the same days last year have data, so there is no total to show."; the alternative, a second Monday-to-Sunday summary for the tile alone, would make the tile and the strip disagree. `homeCalendarRange` keeps its `from` (2 years and 4 weeks before this week's Monday, which the month's day detail needs for its "Two years ago" of the month's earlier days) and reads to the later of the month's last day and today plus 6. *Claude's call: unchanged.* Last week (the Monday-to-Sunday week before this one), Who's in town's "This week" group (term runs that start by Sunday), the bar-order line ("Last year this week, Thursday to Sunday brought 58% of the week's guests.", `lateWeekShare`, last year's Monday-to-Sunday week) and the "This week" outline and key of Home's and the Calendar's month grids stay as they are: the owner's decision named the strip and the week view, and the grids' weeks are their rows. This leaves a gap the owner should see: Last week ends on the Sunday before this week's Monday and the strip starts on today, so this week's days before today (on Sunday, Nov 15, 2026: Nov 9 to 14) show in neither block, only in the month grid. *Alternative:* make Last week "The last 7 days" (the 7 days before today), which mirrors the strip; the [CONFIRM] below names both.

### 5. New pages open at the top (request 5)

Owner, 2026-11-15: "if i click a button that navigates to another page, if that button is not supposed to scroll down, then it should show the new page at the top".

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [A `Modal` inside a `Modal` (Remove's dialog over the Your calendars popover) misbehaves on iOS or traps focus wrongly on web] → `ConfirmDialog` stays a child of the popover's tree, as the RN docs require for nested modals; the native and web suites open Remove from the popover, and the browser and native checks cover it. If it fails on a platform, Remove closes the popover first and opens its dialog, which then returns focus to "Your calendars".
- [A modal popover traps focus where a disclosure didn't: the person can't reach the grid while Your calendars is open] → It closes on Escape, Close, the button or a press outside, and keeps its state, so closing costs nothing; only while an add, an import or a Refresh now runs does it wait, as `ConfirmDialog` does.
- [Placement from `measureInWindow` is wrong after a scroll or rotation] → The page can't scroll under a modal, and placement is measured again on every window size change and every keyboard show or hide.
- [The keyboard covers a field of a popover on a phone] → Below 641px the card is pinned to the top of the window, and at every width its height stops at the keyboard's top (native `Keyboard` events, web `visualViewport`); the device check covers iOS and Android.
- [Week blocks that start on today make a reload days later show different blocks for the same `?day=`] → The block always holds the selected day and today; only its first day moves, which is the owner's request.
- [Reading the week's own 7 days near a grid's edge adds reads when switching views there] → Only when the block runs past the grid's 42 days; elsewhere switching reads nothing, as today.
- [A site changes its logo, or a favicon fetch picks a poor icon] → Logos are reviewed in the PR that runs the script and re-fetched only by running it again; any host can fall back to the globe by removing its PNG.
- [Six graded colors might read as decoration or fail in light mode] → The words say the same, contrast is pinned at 4.5:1, and the owner checks both themes in the browser task.
- [`averageGuests` adds a guest figure to TownwiseAI's tool result] → Guest counts are already in `get_forecast`'s result (days' actuals and ranges); nothing logs tool results.
- [A note's size depends on the model's middle figure, which a closed model day lacks] → Such a note ranks as the largest rise (+100), which is what it does to that day.
- [A buyout smaller than the model's middle shows as a deep red fall (a party of 70 on a middle of 200 is -65), which may surprise the owner] → Its words say so ("Books the day: about 65% fewer guests than Townwise's model"), and the size [CONFIRM] names a buyout's sign.
