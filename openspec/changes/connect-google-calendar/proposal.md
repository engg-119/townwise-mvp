## Why

On 2026-11-15, reviewing the Calendar (P28) in the browser, the owner asked: "i should be able to connect a google calendar". Today a Google calendar reaches Townwise only as a pasted secret iCal address or an exported `.ics` file (connect-owner-calendar, D33), which owners have to dig out of Google's settings, and whose repeating events are counted but never imported. Signing in with Google and picking calendars is the way an owner expects to do it.

Owner decisions, 2026-11-15: sign in with Google, read-only OAuth access, the owner picks which calendars to keep, and Google's access is stored sealed as calendar links are today. The owner accepted that read-only calendar access is a Google-reviewed (sensitive) scope: until Google approves the app, owners see Google's "unverified app" warning and at most 100 people can connect.

## What Changes

- **A third calendar kind, `google`.** A location's up to 5 calendars (D33) can now include Google calendars, beside imported files and links. They reuse phase 1's store, slots, generations, statuses, refresh loop, "Refresh now", calendar key and audit events.
- **Connect with Google (web).** In the Calendar's "Your calendars" popover (built by `refine-calendar-and-home`, request 1, which this change builds on), "Add a Google calendar" explains what Townwise reads, opens Google's own window (Google Identity Services, code flow, popup), and then shows a picker of the person's Google calendars to add to the location. Native lists, refreshes and removes Google calendars but connects on the web only.
- **Read-only, narrowest scopes.** Townwise asks for `openid`, `calendar.calendarlist.readonly` and `calendar.events.readonly`, and reads only each event's id, status, title, start and end. Repeating events are expanded by Google, so they are kept, unlike files and links.
- **Google's access is a secret.** The refresh token is sealed with AES-256-GCM under `TOWNWISE_CALENDAR_KEY`, as a link's address is, and is never answered, logged or audited; Google calendar ids are sealed too. No access token is stored.
- **One connection per person, ended when it is no longer needed.** A person connects the Google account they sign in to Townwise with. Townwise revokes the grant at Google and deletes it when the person's last Google calendar is removed, when they cancel the picker without adding one (or leave it unused for a day), when they leave or are removed from the organization, and when the location or organization is deleted. Each step marks the connection for a check before it acts, every Google read first checks the person's seat, and every connection is checked daily, so an interrupted request never leaves a departed person's calendars being read.
- **Staff view-as shows no Google calendar's name or event title.** Google's policy lets people read Google user data only with "the user's affirmative agreement to view specific messages, files, or other data", so under view-as a Google calendar shows as "Google calendar" and its events as "Google calendar event".
- **TownwiseAI keeps no calendar events in saved conversations.** A `get_owner_calendar` result is saved as a stub with counts only, as `read_data_item`'s is (D28), so event titles reach Anthropic only in the turn that read them. Its name, description and input are unchanged.
- **New routes.** `org.google_calendars.connect`, `org.google_calendars.list` (the picker's choices) and `org.google_calendars.disconnect` (an unused connection), with a new `townwise:google-calendars` limiter. `org.owner_calendars.create` takes `{ kind: 'google', googleCalendarId }`, `refresh` takes Google calendars, `remove` ends idle access, and the list answer gains `google: { available, connected, clientId }`.
- **New collection** `tw_google_connections`, and new statuses (`google_revoked`, `google_not_found`, `google_forbidden`, `google_rate_limited`, `google_unavailable`).
- **Off by default.** A new Terraform flag, `enable_google_calendar` (default false, and only with `enable_calendar_links`), adds the client secret from Key Vault; a runbook step sets up Google Cloud (the Calendar API, the consent screen's scopes, publishing status, verification).
- **Docs and privacy.** Spec D39 (new), D33, 10.18, P28, 17, the test lists and the CONFIRM index; `api/README.md`; `docs/portal/tuning-chat.md` (`get_owner_calendar` reads Google calendars, with no description change, and its saved result is a stub); `client/MODULE.md`; the privacy page, which gains Google's Limited Use statement and says what Townwise reads from Google and who can see it.
- This reverses connect-owner-calendar's non-goals "Google Calendar sign-in (OAuth)" and, for Google calendars only, "Expanding repeating events". That change stays as written; this one records the reversal.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `google-calendar`: connecting a Google account by signing in with Google, the scopes and what Townwise reads, sealing Google's access, the picker and the add, reading and refreshing Google calendars, ending access and revoking the grant, staff visibility and the person's agreement, the config flag, and the routes' limits, audit events and logs.

### Modified Capabilities
- `owner-calendar` (from the unarchived `connect-owner-calendar` change, its Calendar page requirement as `refine-calendar-and-home` modifies it): "A location keeps its calendars" (a `google` kind), "Only an event's title, dates and hours are stored" (Google's repeating events kept), "Calendars are managed by members and isolated by organization" (connecting is a member's own, never staff's), "The calendar list never returns a subscription address" (nor a Google calendar id or token; the list gains `google`), and "The Calendar page shows and manages stored calendars" ("Add a Google calendar" and its picker in the popover).
- `calendar-subscription` (from `connect-owner-calendar`): "Refresh now" also refreshes a Google calendar.
- `townwise-ai-owner-calendar` (from `connect-owner-calendar`): "The tool's views and logs carry no titles" (the saved conversation keeps a stub of each calendar read, for every kind).

## Impact

- **API** (`api/src`): new `calendar/google.ts` (a fixed-host Google client), `calendar/google-events.ts`, `calendar/google-access.ts`, `stores/google-connections.ts` and `handlers/google-calendars.ts`; changes to `calendar/ics.ts` (shared helpers exported), `calendar/refresher.ts`, `stores/owner-calendars.ts`, `stores/cross-tenant.ts`, `stores/index.ts`, `stores/audit-events.ts`, `audit.ts`, `handlers/owner-calendars.ts`, the seat-loss callers (`handlers/team.ts`, `handlers/org.ts`, `handlers/admin.ts`), `handlers/locations.ts`, `handlers/deletion.ts`, `access/routes.ts`, `chat/tools/owner-calendar.ts` (its saved stub only), `config.ts`, `main.ts`, `app.ts`, `.env.example` and `production.env`. No change to `api/kit/*`.
- **Client** (`client/src`): `portal/calendar/api.ts`, `calendars.ts`, `YourCalendars.tsx`, `CalendarPage.tsx`, new `GoogleConnect.web.tsx` and `GoogleConnect.tsx`, and a new shared GIS loader `auth/gis.web.ts` that `auth/GoogleButton.web.tsx` moves to. The privacy page.
- **Infra**: one new Key Vault secret, `townwise-google-calendar-secret`, behind `enable_google_calendar`; `variables.tf`, `main.tf`, `tests/production.tftest.hcl`; `infra/README.md` step 22. The CSP is expected to stay as it is (Google's script is already allowed and its window is a popup); the browser check confirms it.
- **Google Cloud** (owner): enable the Google Calendar API in the OAuth project, add the two scopes to the consent screen, keep it "In production" (the status is the whole project's, sign-in's included), add a new secret to the web client (Google shows a secret in full only when it is created), and submit the app for verification (home page, privacy policy, domain verification, scope justifications, a demo video; up to about 10 days).
- **Privacy and cost**: Google event titles join the stored calendar titles that TownwiseAI can send to Anthropic; Townwise's server calls Google's APIs (free under the quotas); Google sees the Calendar visit when the owner opens "Add a Google calendar".
- **No new npm dependency.** Depends on `refine-calendar-and-home` (its popover) and `connect-owner-calendar`, and is archived after both.
