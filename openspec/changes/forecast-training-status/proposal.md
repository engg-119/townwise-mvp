## Why

On 2026-11-19 the owner asked: "is there some way as a user to see when an agent is retraining a model or to view past runs or to view the live view of a model training? where should this be? on the dashboard page that links to a full page, during onboarding (technically shouldn't the research agent work in the background then it should let the user know once it is ready, same for any model training / experimenting?" The owner approved the agreed shape ("build both end to end"): an updating state, a run history in TownwiseAI's activity, a first forecast built in the background with a one-time email, and no live training view.

Today owners see none of it. A calibration (spec 6.23) runs for minutes, started by the weekly refresh, a D59 refit after a sales upload or a TownwiseAI change, an org admin's request, or staff, and the owner's pages say nothing until a run is published. A run the gate keeps as a draft, or one that fails, leaves no trace an owner can read: `GATE_WORDS` and `FAILURE_WORDS` are staff words, shown only in the console. The calibration state (14.1) is derived from sales and published runs only; 14.3 reserves `queued`, `calibrating` and `failed`. Nothing records what started a system calibration (the weekly refresh and a D59 refit are both `system`/`scheduled`), or how many days it used.

The first forecast already starts on its own after an onboarding upload: every applied upload marks the location as wanting a refit (D59), and the hourly worker pass starts it after 15 quiet minutes, once the history spans 230 days, the organization is active with forecasts on and the lab runner and weekly schedule are on. So usually up to about 75 minutes, longer when a cap holds the start, pass with Home saying only "Townwise hasn't published a forecast", and nobody is told when it lands.

By the owner's rule of 2026-11-16, features the owner asks for ship on by default. Spec decision **D65** (D64 is PR #126; D65 and D66 were claimed for this pair on 2026-11-19).

## What Changes

- **Updating, as an additive field.** Every calibration answer (GET /api/org's locations, the sales GET, the upload answer) gains `updating: { stage, since } | null`. `running` while a calibration of the location is queued or running and younger than 20 minutes; `waiting` while a location with no published run and no succeeded calibration has a refit mark the hourly pass will take (the pass's own 24-hour rule, conditions and 230 days). The four `state` values do not change, so an older client still parses every answer; `queued`, `calibrating` and `failed` stay reserved. The read never writes: a calibration past its 20 minutes reads as not updating, and as a failed run, without `expireStale`.
- **The last run, as a server fact.** `not_calibrated` gains `lastRun: { outcome, reason, finishedAt } | null`, so Home can say why a first forecast was not published, and what happens next, whenever the owner comes back.
- **Where it shows.** The model card: "Updating your forecast with 6 new days, started 2:14 PM." Home, for a location with no published run: "The first forecast is next" while queued, "Building the first forecast" while it runs, then the forecast once it is published, with no reload, or a notice saying why it was not. The dashboard's Calibration cell (P9, two or more locations) gets a second line in plain words, linked to TownwiseAI's activity (`?panel=activity`). The upload result says the first forecast is next. TownwiseAI's overview tool says it too. Pages poll quietly every 30 seconds while something is updating and never flash a skeleton.
- **What started it.** A system calibration stores `causes`: `weekly`, `sales`, `person_change` or `townwise_ai`. The refit mark (`tw_locations.refit`) gains `causes` with `$addToSet`, so every caller of `wantRefit` names its cause and the hourly pass carries them onto the calibration. A person's start reads `staff` when Townwise staff started it, by any route, else `asked`. `via` itself does not change, so the console, the infra pins and the `forecasts_failed` alert are untouched.
- **What it used.** `recordInput` stores `dayCount`, `dataFrom` and `newDays` (days after the newest published run's last day) beside `dataThrough`.
- **Run history.** A new activity source, `forecast_made`, appended to `ACTIVITY_SOURCES`: each recently finished calibration of the location (the newest 5 always kept) with its causes, the days it used, an owner outcome (`published`, `kept`, `not_run`) taken from what happened to its run, and an owner reason code, failures included. No accuracy and no guest figure in any item, so the item is the same under view-as and open-as-admin (D7). Owner words are new, with first-forecast variants; the staff `GATE_WORDS` stay staff-only.
- **The first-forecast email.** A third email, `first_forecast`, to every active org admin of the organization, once per location, when the location's first run is ever published (by the lab or by staff). A compare-and-set marker `tw_locations.firstForecastMailedAt` makes it at most once; it is claimed only when there is an admin to send to. Sending never throws into the publish. No figure, no name in the subject. `EmailRef.requestId` becomes optional and gains `locationId`. The privacy page, D14 and the mailer's header are rewritten to match.
- **One pattern for running and done.** A client hook that polls while something runs and calls back when it ends, and a server rule (an additive `{ stage, since } | null` field on the read the page already makes, plus an activity kind when it finishes, linked with `?panel=activity`). D66's town research reuses both and adds its own kinds, under the isolation constraints in design decision 10.
- **Not built:** a live training view (owners can't use a loss curve; staff keep D50's post-hoc RunTraining), a separate run-history page, starting the first forecast sooner than the hourly pass, and emails for later runs.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `forecast-update-status`: the `updating` field, its stages, the read-only timeout, where it shows, the quiet poll, and the running-and-done pattern D66 reuses.
- `forecast-run-history`: causes on refit marks and calibrations, the days a calibration used, the `forecast_made` activity kind, owner outcomes and words, and D7.
- `first-forecast-notice`: Home's first-forecast flow and the one-time email to org admins.

### Modified Capabilities
None in `openspec/specs/`, which holds only `order-setup-read` and `top-bar-groups`. This change extends unarchived changes (`autonomous-forecasts` for 6.23 and the weekly refresh, `auto-apply-agent-reads` for refit marks and the activity). As they did, its requirements are written as new ones so they validate on their own.
