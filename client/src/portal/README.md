# The portal: shared pieces and page ownership

The signed-in app of `docs/portal/spec.md`: access routing (4.3), the app shell (15), and every page in 16.1. Each page owns its route file plus a folder of its own under `src/portal/`; change a page there, and say so in your PR if you have to edit anything shared.

## How routing works

- `app/(gate)/(app)/_layout.tsx` wraps every signed-in screen in `PortalProvider` and `PortalGate`. The provider reads `GET /api/me` once per sign-in. Until it answers, the page says “Checking your access”; if it fails, the shared error with Try again.
- Each group's layout (`(access)`, `(client)`, `(account)`, `(console)`) wraps its pages in `AccessGuard`, which redirects any path the person may not open to their state's own route (`routing.ts`: `HOME`, `isAllowed`). A page therefore never mounts in the wrong state, so it can assume its state (narrow `me.access` and return an empty view otherwise, as the gate pages do).
- `isAllowed` also gates by role (`/team`, `/locations/new` and a location's Settings tab are for org admins) and never allows the console or `/settings/security` during view-as. A new path must be added there, or it redirects.
- `usePortal().api` re-reads `GET /api/me` on 403 `no_access`, `forbidden`, `staff_setup_required`, `staff_reauth_required` and 409 `view_as_active` and `open_as_admin_active`, and clears the cached organization, so the guard routes again. Use it for every `/api` call. `applyMe(me)` takes any answer that carries the access (join, decline, a request, End view-as).
- Staff in view-as get the target's access from `/api/me`, so they are routed as the target, under the banner.
- Staff open as admin (`me.openAsAdmin`) get the organization admin's access, so they are routed as that admin under `OpenAsAdminBanner`, with no Team (`isAllowed`, `clientNav`) and no Leave section. A `GET /api/me` read that finds the mode gone sends End once (`PortalProvider.refreshMe`), and Sign out sends End before it signs out (`useSignOut` in `shell/Frame.tsx`). End, from either banner, sends the grant id the page shows, and renaming the organization and adding a location send its organization id (`openAsAdminOrgId`), so a tab left on an earlier organization is refused (409 `open_as_admin_active`) instead of acting on the current one.

## Frames and page layout

- Frames are in the group layouts: the gate frame, the client app frame (top bar with the organization, the nav per role, the email and Sign out), the account frame (client app for members, gate otherwise) and the staff console (web only; native shows one line). Pages render only their page.
- Non-location pages render `<Page eyebrow title lead? side? gate?>` from `shell/Page.tsx`: the header, the one-time notice, then your content. `side` holds the page's actions (at most one primary) or a gate page's side column.
- Location tab pages render only their content: the location layout (`shell/LocationShell.tsx`) already draws the header, the switcher and the tabs. Read the location with `useLocation()` (`{ location, org }`).
- Member pages name a location through `shownLocationName(location, org)` (`locations/naming.ts`, D29): the organization's name with one location, the location's own with several. A sentence that names the location uses it, not `location.name`; lists of several locations, the upload's column match and the staff console use stored names.
- The chat route fills the screen instead of scrolling (`routing.ts` `isFillRoute`).

## Shared pieces

- `PortalProvider.tsx`: `usePortal()` (`me`, `api`, `applyMe`, `refreshMe`, `showNotice`), `useOrg()` (`GET /api/org`, loaded once and shared), and `useReadOnly()` (`{ readOnly, line }`: under view-as, render every write control disabled and show `line` once per form or section, tied by `describedBy`).
- `hooks.ts`: `useLoad(load)` for reads (wrap `load` in `useCallback`; the state is `loading`, `failed` or `ready`, with `reload`), `useAction(fn)` for writes (one at a time, with `pending` and `error`), and `useBreakpoint()` (phone at 640px and below, desktop from 1024px).
- `errors.ts`: `errorCopy(error)` (the shared copy of spec 15.7), `hasCode(error, status, code)` for your page's own codes, `COPY`.
- `api.ts` and `schemas.ts`: zod schemas for the shapes the API returns. Add your routes' schemas to `schemas.ts` and their calls to `api.ts` (or a file of your own); `z.object` strips unknown keys.
- `format.ts`: `formatDate` (“Mar 2, 2026”), `formatTime` (“3:42 PM”), `formatCount`, `plural`, `townLabel`, `roleLabel`.
- `towns.ts`: the twelve towns from the homepage's bundled data, `townProfile`, `townResearchLine` (P9), `townSentences`. `town/TownProfile.tsx` is the whole profile (chart on web, text and table on native) for P15.
- `AddLocationForm.tsx`: Name and Town, or the places checklist first while the Places search is on (a Name per checked place, from Google's), one `POST /api/org/locations` per location in order with each row's outcome on its row, then the Home of the first one added (P10). P21's staff setup passes a `target` instead: its own route, what follows once a press has added locations, the search's availability and its limit line.
- `moments/` (import from `src/portal/moments`): the statement moments (spec 15.8). `PortalGate` mounts `MomentProvider` once, around the `Slot`, after `GET /api/me` answers. It is the one player and decides request received and the welcome itself (`momentFor`), so no page mounts it or plays those two. A page may call `useMoment().play(statement)` for a statement of its own (dropped while one is playing) and hold its own entrance while `useMoment().playing`.
- `onboarding/HistoryStep.tsx`: onboarding step 3 on Home, the drop and Reading screen, over `files/` (the upload queue, the import poller and `ImportQuestion`). Beside it, `HistoryStepDrop` is the drop zone and file picker on web (native has neither and says where to drop files) and `historyMotion` grows Home's history card from where the Reading calendar was (web; nothing moves on native). `history.ts` holds the step's pure rules and copy, with `needsHistory(org, locations)`. Home imports the step and the rule only from `home/onboarding.ts`.
- `onboarding/PlaceSearch.tsx` (native, each suggestion a button) and `PlaceSearch.web.tsx` (an ARIA combobox), sharing `PlaceSearchParts.tsx`: the Google Places search that P16 and the console's setup Rename mount (P3 and P10 use the places checklist below). `usePlaceSearch.ts` runs it (250 ms debounce, 2 characters, one call in flight, one session token per search, which the pick's details call ends with `endSession`), and `usePlaceSearchAvailability` says whether to offer it: P3 asks with one autocomplete `{}`, P10 and P16 read `placeSearch` from `GET /api/org`, and under view-as it is off and nothing is sent. `places.ts` holds the Places calls and the three writes that carry a confirmed place (`sendAccessRequestAt`, `createLocationAt`, `setLocationPlace`), so `api.ts` and `locations/api.ts` keep theirs unchanged.
- `onboarding/usePlaceChecklist.ts`, `PlaceChecklist.tsx` (each suggestion a checkbox), `PlaceChecklist.web.tsx` (an ARIA combobox with a multiselectable listbox) and `PlaceChecklistParts.tsx`: the places checklist for P3 and P10, the search with up to 25 places checked from any of its answers, kept in check order below the field. The hook runs the search, asks Google for each checked place (the first check of a search ends its session; later ones carry no token), and says why a place wasn't checked; its `map` (`places/usePlacesMap.ts`) is one map of every checked place, drawn under the field and above the rows. `rowExtra` adds a page's own part to each checked row. `checklist.ts` holds its limit (`MAX_CHECKED_PLACES`) and words, with Add location's primary (`addLabel`).
- `places/`: `PlaceCard` (“Here’s what we found”, with the map at its top), `PlaceSummary` (the confirmed place in a form) and `PlaceRow` (a checked place in the checklist, with no map of its own, and `PLACES_SOURCE` under the list); `PlaceMap`, the signed Maps Static image the browser or app loads straight from Google (nothing at all with no map or after a failed load), with `map.ts` (`placeMapSchema`) and `usePlacesMap.ts` (the checklist's map of every checked place, from `POST /api/places/map`); and `focus.ts` (`useFocusOnMount`, `SCRIPT_FOCUS`), which moves focus as one step of the search replaces another.
- `upload/useSalesFile.ts`: P13's daily-totals reader (choose, match columns, check), which never keeps the file's text; `upload/UploadPage.web.tsx` and P21's `console/SetupUpload.web.tsx` mount it with their own send. `MatchStep` takes `cancelHref` only on P13, and the staff upload passes `locationColumn={false}`, so no column's cell values are ever listed there.
- `ui/` (import from `src/portal/ui`): `Button` and `LinkButton` (primary white, outline, link; `disabled` keeps the same element and its focus, with aria-disabled on web), `Actions`, `FormField` (`alert` when its messages come back from a submit, as a wrong code does), `Select`, `TownFields` with `checkTown`, `DataTable` (stacks at 640px and below; from 641px to 1023px it keeps a minimum width, `MIN_FLEX_WIDTH` per unit of column flex, and its region scrolls sideways; from 1024px it fills its column; give a column of emails `EMAIL_FLEX`) and `DefinitionList`, `ConfirmDialog` (with `typedName`, and `onDismiss` once it has gone and returned focus; Cancel and Escape wait while its action runs), `MessageFocus` and `useFocusMessage()` (when an action removes its opener, focus moves to the resulting `Notice` or `ErrorNotice`), `Notice`, `ErrorNotice`, `LoadingLine`, `EmptyState`, `ReadOnlyLine`, `StatusTag`, `Panel`, `Section`, `Columns`, `Pair`, `Stack`, and the type components (`Title`, `Heading`, `Body`, `Small`, `Mono`, `Eyebrow`), with `inlineTarget` for a link inside a sentence (a 44px tap target on web that leaves the line where it was).
- `ui/hover.ts`: a child of expo-router's `<Link asChild>` gets its style merged as one object, so a function style is dropped and an array throws. Give such a child one flattened plain style (`StyleSheet.flatten(...)`, a `styles.x` member or an object literal) and take hover from `useHover()` instead of a style function, as `LinkButton` and the location tabs do. `test/portal/link-child.test.ts` fails any other style.

Mint (`#7bd2b4`) is for data and selection only, never a button or a message. Visible copy uses curly apostrophes and no em dashes. Nothing animates on keyboard input.

## Tests

`client/test/helpers/portal.ts` has fixtures in the API's shapes for every access state (`ACCESS`, `meOf`, `staffMe`, `viewAsMe`, `orgOf`) and `fakePortalApi`, which answers `/api/me`, `/api/org` and any write you add to `answers`. `renderApp(path, api)` (`helpers/renderApp.tsx`) renders the whole app on native; on web, use `appRoutes(seededRoot(auth))` with `ExpoRoot`, as `test/app/shell.test.web.tsx` does. Add your route to `ROUTES` in `helpers/routes.ts`; `test/marketing/links.test.ts` keeps it equal to the files in `app/`.
