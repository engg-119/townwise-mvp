# Booking rule for the Oct 22 to Dec 31, 2026 forecasts

Registered 2026-11-01, before any sales from Nov 2 onward existed. It changes how the frozen v3 guest forecast is presented and scored on booked nights; the forecast files themselves are unchanged.

## Why

A private buyout makes a busy night look empty in Toast: the guests are there, but they're billed on an invoice instead of rung up as walk-ins. Kieran knows these nights in advance, so the forecast shouldn't try to predict walk-ins on them.

## Rule

A booked event is a **full buyout** if any of these hold:

1. It's on a day Sawtooth is normally closed (Sunday, or a Monday from May 25, 2026), so the bar opens only for the event.
2. It's a reunion.
3. It's a formal on a Friday or Saturday.

On full-buyout nights the forecast shows "booked: private event" and makes no walk-in forecast. Every other booked night keeps the v3 forecast, with a note. Kieran's own calendar (buyout yes or no) overrides the rule once we have it.

Bookings come from Toast until then: dated Event-category item names and invoice numbers, which encode the event date (for example, Invoice #10042502 is Oct 4, 2025). Off-site catering is excluded.

## How well it fits history

On the 49 past booked days, the rule flags 9 as buyouts. Of the 14 booked days where walk-ins fell below 0.6x a normal night, it catches 6; 2 of the days it flags had normal walk-in traffic. The rule was written after looking at those bookings, so this fit is optimistic. It is a weak stand-in for Kieran's calendar.
